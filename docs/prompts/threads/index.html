The course scenario

The Lumo Cold Kopi launch

The situation
It is late October 2026. Lumo Kopi Co. launches Lumo Cold Kopi, its first ready-to-drink canned kopi, in Singapore on 1 December — six weeks away — and in Kuala Lumpur in January. The marketing team is small, the budget is S$60,000, and nothing has been produced yet: no research, no deck, no posts, no video.
Your role
You are the Lumo marketing team. Instead of juggling a dozen separate chats, you run the whole launch from one Claude Project. Its lead agent plans each job, starts parallel threads for the pieces, and brings the results back for you to check and approve.
The mission
By the end of the day the project holds everything the launch needs — verified market research, a dashboard, a deck and brief, a brand-voice skill, two weeks of social posts, a launch video — and scheduled tasks that keep it moving while you are away.

Steps and prompts — Create the launch project

This lab has three blocks. Block 1 is the project goal, pasted into the New project dialog. Block 2 is the project instructions, pasted into Project settings. Block 3 is your first message to the lead agent.

  1. Download the course labs and open the Lab 01 data/brand-kit folder.

    Skim the three files — guidelines, product sheet, launch brief. You will check Claude's answers against them.

  2. In the Claude desktop app, open Projects from the sidebar and click New project.

    Projects are shared across the Claude app (Chat and Cowork) and Claude Code, so the same project is reachable from all three.

  3. Name the project Lumo Cold Kopi Launch and paste Block 1 into the Goal field. Prompt below

    The goal is what the lead agent steers every decision toward, so it is worth writing as an outcome with a date and a measure.

    Lab 1 Block 1 — GOAL

    Paste this into the Goal field of the New project dialog:

    Launch Lumo Cold Kopi in Singapore on 1 December 2026.
Produce the market research, a launch deck, a campaign brief,
two weeks of social media posts and a 15-second launch video,
within a S$60,000 budget, so that 25-34 year-olds on Instagram
and TikTok know the product exists by launch day.

    Name the project Lumo Cold Kopi Launch, add the three brand-kit files as Context, and click Create project.

  4. Under Context, add the three brand-kit files, then click Create project.

    Context is read, never edited — the right home for reference files you want kept exactly as they are.

  5. Click the gear icon to open Project settings, choose Memory, and paste Block 2 into Project instructions. Save. Prompt below

    Instructions are standing rules for every thread. Short, checkable rules work better than general wishes.

    Lab 1 Block 2 — INSTRUCTIONS

    Paste this into Project settings → Memory → Project instructions:

    You are the marketing team for Lumo Kopi Co.
- Prices are in Singapore dollars: S$3.90 per can, S$42.00 per
  12-pack. Always two decimals.
- Follow lumo-brand-guidelines.md for anything customer-facing.
- Never describe the product as healthy. You may say 5 g sugar.
- Never send an email or publish a post without my approval.
- When a job has several independent subjects, give each one
  its own thread and merge the results for me.
- Flag any figure that two sources disagree on. Do not pick one.

    Save the instructions, then go back to the project and send Block 3.

  6. While you are in settings, open General and look at Models.

    Note the model and effort for the lead agent and for threads. You will see their cost in Lab 10.

  7. Back in the project, send Block 3 to the lead agent. Prompt below

    This is a setup check: if it cannot restate the goal or find the files, fix that now, before any real work.

    Lab 1 Block 3 — FIRST MESSAGE

    Send this to the lead agent:

    Before we start work, check your setup:
1. Restate the project goal in one line.
2. List the context files you can read and what each is for.
3. List the three instructions you think matter most.
4. From the product sheet, give me the price per can, the sugar
   per can and the launch date.
Then remember this for the project: our primary audience is
25-34 year-olds in Singapore, and our secondary market is
Kuala Lumpur, launching 15 January 2027.

    Compare the answer with the brand kit, then open Project settings → Memory and find the new memory entry.

  8. Check the answers: S$3.90 per can, 5 g sugar, launch 1 December 2026.

    Every figure must match the product sheet. A wrong figure here means a wrong context file.

  9. Open Project settings → Memory and find the memory entry about the audience and Kuala Lumpur.

    The project wrote it, so you are responsible for it. Memory you never read is memory you cannot trust.

Check — done when

The Lumo Cold Kopi Launch project exists with the goal from Block 1, the three brand-kit files under Context and the Block 2 instructions saved. The lead agent restated the goal, listed all three context files, and quoted S$3.90 per can, 5 g sugar and a 1 December 2026 launch. Run Market Research in Parallel Threads

Scenario
Before anyone writes a post, the team needs to know the market. Four subjects need research — competitors, the customer survey, pricing and social trends — and none depends on another. You give the whole job to the lead agent in one message. It starts one thread per subject, each with its own data file, then merges the four findings into one research brief, which you check against the data.
Objective
By the end of this lab you can hand a multi-subject research job to the lead agent and verify the merged brief.
You will build
A research brief (lumo-research-brief.docx) merged from four parallel threads, saved in the project. Uses: Claude Projects, lead agent and threads, web search
Data files
lumo-consumer-survey-2026.csv, lumo-competitors.csv, lumo-social-trends.csv
In the project
Instructions — HOW: Uses: one thread per subject, and flag any figure two sources disagree on.
Context — WHAT: Uses: the launch brief, so every thread knows the product, price and audience.
Folder — WHERE: Adds: attach the three CSVs; lumo-research-brief.docx is saved in the project.
Scheduled — WHEN: Not used — the four threads run now, in parallel.

Before you start

Note: Attach the three CSV files from the Lab 02 data folder, or add them to the project's Context first.

Steps and prompts — Research the market in four threads

  1. Open the Lab 02 data folder and glance at the three CSV files.

    Know your source: 400 survey rows, six competitors, twelve content themes.

  2. In the Lumo Cold Kopi Launch project, attach the three files and send the lab prompt. Prompt below

    One message, four subjects. You are briefing the lead agent, not doing the splitting yourself.

    Lab 2 Prompt
    Research the market for the Lumo Cold Kopi launch. The data is in
the three files I have attached. Split this into four threads,
one per subject, and run them in parallel:

Thread 1 - COMPETITORS (lumo-competitors.csv): price per can, pack,
  positioning and channel for each brand. Where is the gap Lumo
  can own?
Thread 2 - CUSTOMERS (lumo-consumer-survey-2026.csv): share likely
  to buy (intent 4 or 5), split by age band and city; top purchase
  drivers; top discovery channels.
Thread 3 - PRICING: where S$3.90 sits against the competitor
  median, and the share of respondents willing to pay S$3.50 or
  more.
Thread 4 - SOCIAL TRENDS (lumo-social-trends.csv): the three content
  themes with the highest engagement, with one post idea each.

When all four threads are done, merge them into one research brief,
lumo-research-brief.docx: a one-paragraph summary, one section per
thread with the key numbers, and three recommendations for the
launch. Show your working for every percentage. Tell me which
thread produced each section, and flag anything the threads
disagree on.

  3. Watch the lead agent announce the threads it starts.

    Each thread has one subject and one file — a focused brief with a fresh context window. That is why parallel research stays sharp.

  4. Open one thread while it works and read its brief.

    You can see exactly what the lead agent asked each thread to do. A poor result usually traces back to a poor thread brief.

  5. When the merged brief arrives, check the survey numbers.

    Expect 400 respondents, 248 likely to buy (62%), and 112 of 152 in the 25-34 band (74%).

  6. Check the channel and driver findings.

    Instagram is the top discovery channel at 136 (34%), then TikTok at 104 (26%). Taste is the top driver at 124 (31%).

  7. Check the pricing section.

    The competitor median is S$4.30, so S$3.90 sits below it; 228 respondents (57%) would pay S$3.50 or more.

  8. Check the trends section and the recommendations.

    The top theme should be Taste tests and first-sip reactions. Do the three recommendations follow from the numbers above them?

  9. Open lumo-research-brief.docx and confirm each section names its thread.

    Traceability: every number in the brief can be traced to one thread and one file.

Check — done when

lumo-research-brief.docx has a summary, four thread sections and three recommendations. It reports 400 respondents, 248 likely to buy (62%), 112 of 152 aged 25-34 (74%), Instagram as the top discovery channel at 34%, Taste as the top driver at 31%, a competitor median of S$4.30 with Lumo's S$3.90 below it, 228 respondents (57%) willing to pay S$3.50 or more, and Taste tests and first-sip reactions as the top content theme.

Steps and prompts — Build the Market Insights dashboard

  1. In the project, send the lab prompt with the two CSV files attached or in Context. Prompt below

    The project already knows the brand colours from the guidelines in Context — you do not attach them again.

    Lab 3 Prompt
    Using lumo-consumer-survey-2026.csv and lumo-competitors.csv, build
a Live Artefact: an interactive Market Insights dashboard for the
Lumo Cold Kopi launch.

Include:
- KPI tiles: respondents, % likely to buy (intent 4 or 5), top
  discovery channel, competitor median price.
- A bar chart of discovery channels.
- A bar chart of purchase drivers.
- A price ladder: the six competitors and Lumo at S$3.90, cheapest
  to dearest, with Lumo highlighted.
- A filter for city: All, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur. The KPI tiles and
  both survey charts must follow the filter.

Bake the data into the artefact - do not fetch anything. Use the
Lumo brand colours from the brand guidelines. Title it
"Lumo Cold Kopi - Market Insights".

  2. Check the KPI tiles with the filter on All.

    400 respondents, 62% likely to buy, Instagram top channel, competitor median S$4.30 — the same numbers as the Lab 2 brief.

Check — done when

The Market Insights artefact shows 400 respondents, 62% likely to buy, Instagram as the top channel and a S$4.30 competitor median. The city filter changes the respondent count to 240 (Singapore) and 160 (Kuala Lumpur) and both survey charts follow it. The price ladder runs from S$3.20 to S$5.60 with Lumo highlighted at S$3.90.
